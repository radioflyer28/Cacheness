## Decisions Made

- Production code has no boto3 dependency or import; boto3 remains limited to moto-qualified development fixture administration.
- Native Amazon S3 requires explicit bucket and region. Stable bucket-name ownership plus narrowly scoped IAM and bucket policy are the D-16 controls; `ExpectedBucketOwner`, custom signing, custom production endpoints, and boto3 fallback remain unsupported.
- Direct conditional create remains capped at a configurable 128 MiB initial default. Canonical SHA-256 plus byte size is verified before deserialization; ETag/version are signed opaque observations for read-only comparison only.
- The custom path-handler registration seam remains `store.handlers.register_handler(...)` with `CacheHandler.put/get(Path, ...)`; handlers receive no obstore object or managed locator.
- Real AWS, live PostgreSQL, native platforms, the independent full optional-group matrix, RSS/performance budgets, and SHA-256-versus-XXH3 benchmarks remain explicit Phase 8 gates.
