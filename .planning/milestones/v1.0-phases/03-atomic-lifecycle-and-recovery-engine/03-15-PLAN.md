Make facade mutations participate in canonical admission and move every destructive projection/link transition behind exact successful authority promotion.

Purpose: The current facade bypasses BlobStore admission, writes derived projection state before promotion, retries against peer candidate state, misclassifies empty canonical stores as legacy, and accepts hostile locator evidence as a mutation token. Closing those paths restores old-or-new generation behavior and recoverable failures under STOR-03 through STOR-07.
Output: Exactly-once put admission, bounded clear-snapshot admission, pure pre-promotion projection preparation, exact post-promotion projection ownership, fail-closed canonical cleanup, and deterministic adversarial facade tests.
</objective>
