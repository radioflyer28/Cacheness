<objective>
Close the migration-stage attribution and abort-recovery blocker without changing the single-authority cutover model.

Purpose: Make each bounded run's authority-attributed effects exactly resumable before activation, split larger catalogs into independent runs, and preserve fail-closed visibility across the accepted pre-checkpoint orphan window.

</objective>
