## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-07-17-01 | Information Disclosure | `MigrationPlan.canonical_bytes` | high | mitigate | Replace raw catalog/manifest values with canonical digests and regression-test a unique sensitive sentinel across every shareable byte path. |
| T-07-17-02 | Tampering | plan-bound source state | high | mitigate | Re-read through the authenticated source boundary and compare canonical catalog/manifest digests before candidate mutation. |
| T-07-17-03 | Spoofing | source manifest authentication | high | mitigate | Require the existing integrity verifier before accepting a digest comparison; caller-supplied unauthenticated objects cannot authorize execution. |
| T-07-17-04 | Repudiation | run evidence | medium | mitigate | Persist stable plan/source digests and failure reason codes while excluding secrets. |
| T-07-17-05 | Elevation of Privilege | execution from a stale plan | high | mitigate | Fail closed on any source-state drift and retain D-20's no-adoption rule. |
