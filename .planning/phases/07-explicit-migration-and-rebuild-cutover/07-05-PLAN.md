<objective>
Make every offline action and interruption resumable only from explicit authenticated evidence.

Purpose: Prevent forged progress, stale-plan mutation, secret disclosure, path overlap, and unexplained candidate adoption.
Output: Hardened maintenance evidence codec/store, typed failure vocabulary, and exhaustive security/recovery tests.
</objective>

## Artifacts this phase produces

- `MaintenanceEvidenceState` exact states for inspected, planned, staging, staged, verifying, verified, activated, rolled_back, finalized, purge_pending, purged, and aborted.
- `MaintenanceRunEvidence` fields for version, run/plan digests, source/destination identity and revision, state, ordered completed-step receipts, owned candidate receipt, authority receipts, cleanup debt, and signature metadata.
- `MaintenanceEvidenceStore(work_dir, run_id, key_provider)` with `create()`, `load()`, and exact-byte conditional `checkpoint()`; files are contained beneath the resolved operator work directory.
- Typed `CacheBlobMigrationPlanStaleError`, `CacheBlobMigrationEvidenceError`, `CacheBlobMigrationEvidenceMismatchError`, `CacheBlobMigrationConfirmationError`, and `CacheBlobMigrationCleanupError` (names may align with the existing hierarchy but must preserve these branchable meanings).
- `tests/test_migration_run_evidence.py`.
