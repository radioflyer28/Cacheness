## Critical Anti-Patterns (do NOT repeat these)

- Race-loop coordination: do not introduce another lifecycle authority, lock, admission queue, readiness registry, compatibility projection, or timing-derived correctness rule. Use the existing shared `AuthorityLifecycleEngine` and topology-specific guarantees from ADR 0001.
- Presence ambiguity: do not use bare `None` to mean both a cached value and absence.
- Best-effort destructive policy: do not delete from stale metadata or paths; select authoritatively and delete with exact-generation expectations.
- Compatibility creep: do not preserve pre-production APIs with adapters unless an explicit requirement is added.
- Qualification overclaim: do not mark BACK-05 complete without live PostgreSQL and AWS S3 evidence in Phase 8.
