<objective>
Make clear a bounded resumable aggregate of the common per-entry tombstone lifecycle.

Purpose: Remove whole-store snapshot restoration and protect keys created or changed after the clear target snapshot.
Output: Stable bounded manifest pages, durable clear-target/checkpoint records, aggregate admission, and one authoritative recovery protocol.
</objective>

- New internal `ManifestPage`/opaque cursor primitives in `manifest_repository.py`, exact `ClearTargetPage`/`ClearTargetCheckpoint` evidence in the operation record/repository seam, and `LifecycleEngine.clear`/`resume_clear` methods in `lifecycle.py`.
- New internal `StoreAdmissionBarrier` in `coordination.py`; Plan 03-07 extends this module with refcounted per-key coordination rather than creating the barrier after its consumers.
- Compatibility-only `LegacyClearEvidenceAdapter` in `clear_recovery.py` if old Phase 1 journals must reopen; no new public symbol.
- Expanded `tests/test_blob_store_atomic_lifecycle.py` and `tests/test_clear_recovery.py`; no new files.
